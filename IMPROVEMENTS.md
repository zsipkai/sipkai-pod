# Sipk.ai POD improvement log

Agents add a row here whenever they find a weakness, gap, contradiction or missing check in the method or in a project's rules (R-1.1). Tina keeps the log tidy. Only a named human approves or rejects (R-1.2). `scripts/check-improvements.sh` runs in every gate and refuses an `APPROVED` or `APPLIED` row without a human approver and date, an approver that is an agent or model, and an `APPLIED` row without where it landed.

Status values: `PROPOSED` (an agent suggests it), `APPROVED` (a human said yes), `REJECTED` (a human said no), `APPLIED` (shipped through the docs/process-only lane, evaluations replayed).

Rows SI-001 to SI-155 were written while the method lived in the Money Review repository as `sipkai-sdlc/` (version 1.x) and are carried over unchanged; paths in them refer to that layout.
