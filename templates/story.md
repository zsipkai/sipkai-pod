# STORY-NNN: User-visible outcome, written as a short title

- Status: IDEA | DEFINED | IN DELIVERY | DONE
- Risk: LOW | STANDARD | HIGH | CRITICAL — how wrong it can go; selects the executing model tier (sipkai-pod/RULES.md, R-7.2)
- Size: S | M | L | XL | XXL — how much work; sets the token budget (XXL must be split before planning)
- Proposed execution model: small | mid | top (confirmed by /plan-task, checked by /validate-design; test authoring capped at mid, control and security tests at top)
- Epic: the milestone or theme
- Owner: the human accountable for the outcome
- Surfaces: the parts of the product this touches
- Change record: `backlog/change-records/STORY-NNN.md` (created by /plan-task)
- Coordination issue: optional external issue URL or key

## Story

As a (person), I want (outcome), so that (reason).

Origin: the incident, user report, review finding or strategy this came from.

## Acceptance criteria

Numbered, each one observable and able to fail. Acceptance-critical facts (exact limits, identifiers, copy, rounding) are written here inline; links are for depth, never for facts an implementer would fail review for getting wrong. Mark criteria that can only be proven on a deployed environment with (live).

1. (criterion)
2. (criterion)

## Non-goals

What this story deliberately does not change. Scope creep dies here.

## Dependencies and constraints

- Blocking work: stories, human tasks or decisions that must be done first
- External systems: none, or the list
- Permissions or spending required: none, or what and why
- Rollback boundary: what can be reversed safely, and how

## Open judgment calls

Questions only a human can answer, each with its default if unanswered.

## Ready checklist

- [ ] The problem and intended user outcome are clear.
- [ ] Every acceptance criterion is observable and can fail.
- [ ] Acceptance-critical facts are written inline.
- [ ] Non-goals prevent accidental scope growth.
- [ ] Dependencies, permissions, data and external contracts are named.
- [ ] Risk tier, size and accountable owner are recorded.
- [ ] Open judgment calls are resolved or explicitly listed.
